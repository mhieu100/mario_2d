THE WORLD.

One thumb, one keyboard, one job: get Mario to the flagpole. Movement keys are held, not toggled — let go and he stops on the spot.

THE DECK

Left and right walk, holding B turns a walk into a run. A jumps — tap it lightly for a short hop, hold it for the full arc. The red key restarts the level from the last safe step.
